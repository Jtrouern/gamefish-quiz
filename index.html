Game Fish ID Quiz

Name North America's game fish from live iNaturalist photos and field marks. Species come from FishBase's game-fish list, filtered by state, water type and field-guide section.

Pick your water and cast off

Choose a state or province, freshwater or saltwater, and a field-guide section, then press Load & play. The species list is every fish FishBase flags as a game fish in the US or Canada. A fish is included for your place if it is on FishBase's checklist for it or has iNaturalist observations there (or choose Anywhere). Photos come from that place when possible, otherwise from elsewhere in the species' range. Anadromous fish such as salmon, striped bass and shad count as both fresh and salt.

Keys: 1–6 to answer, H for a hint, P for another photo, F for full size, Enter for the next fish. Click any photo to see it full size.
